HIDDEN TREASURE OF MITHILA

Radha Krishna Dham
Spiritual Temple

Radha Krishna Dham is the spiritual and cultural hub of the Bhakti Movement in Mithila, also known as Radha Krishna Kuti by local devotees. Renowned as one of the most prominent Krishna temples in Mithila, it brings out the timeless wisdom of the Srimad Bhagavata Purana, Chaitanya Charitamrita, as well as authentic practices of Vedic spiritual Culture, into everyday life.

"One who has taken human birth should make their own life successful, and then perform welfare work for the benefit of all other living beings." — Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu (Chaitanya Charitamrita)
