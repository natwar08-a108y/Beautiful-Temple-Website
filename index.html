Nityanand Jayanti:

During the end of January, this festival begins. Major saints from Vrindavan come and participate. A 24-hour Mahamantra Kirtan continues for 15 days. Every year, Bhagwat Katha is performed by Shri Gaurang Das Ji Maharaj. Hundreds of bhaktas are fed Mahaprasad daily. On the final day, a grand Bhandara is held and a Dola Yatra of Thakur Ji moves around the entire village, spreading joy and devotion.
